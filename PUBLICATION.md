# Publication checklist

The public Git history may contain the prompt documentation, reference links, rights notices, and specifically reviewed screenshots under `showcase/`.

Before pushing:

1. Add the direct original page for every entry in `references.md`; include the creator or rights holder and any stated license or terms.
2. Do not commit images obtained through Google Images. A Google Images URL is a discovery trail, not evidence of permission.
3. Keep `reference/`, `runs/`, `pets/`, `previews/`, `showcase-private/`, `design/`, `scripts/`, and `dist/` ignored.
4. Permit only deliberately selected screenshots under `showcase/`; keep spritesheets, frames, references, contact sheets, GIFs, and pet packages excluded.
5. Confirm no prior reachable commit contains the private asset directories or an installable package.
6. Label screenshots as unofficial, unlicensed, non-commercial fan-work examples. Do not describe them as reusable artwork or imply endorsement by the character rights holders or OpenAI.

The screenshots publicly display derivative fan artwork even though they do not distribute the spritesheet. Publication is a case-specific rights decision, and the notice does not create permission from a rights holder.

For a pet intended for broad installation, remixing, or a community gallery, use an original character or character art with express redistribution permission and add an appropriate license for that original work.
